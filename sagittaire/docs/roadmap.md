# Roadmap

sagitt·AI·re is a pro bono project of Ashencroft Studio, built in the open. Here's where it stands.

## Done

- [x] Sessions with any bow, face, number of paper faces and arrows per end, including two 3-spot faces at once
- [x] AI scoring from a photo, with uncertain arrows circled and a one-tap fix
- [x] The line rule and your scoring rule (X = 10, inner 10, X = 11) explained to the AI with every photo
- [x] The AI service of your choice (Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible service at your own address), with your own key
- [x] Scoring without a key: share the photo with the AI app on your phone and paste its answer
- [x] Terms of use and privacy policy in French and English, accepted at first launch, with the open-source licences, in Settings → About and legal
- [x] Erase data (all sessions, or everything), and reset experience and badges from the trophies
- [x] Rename, merge and delete saved places, keeping every session
- [x] Manual scoring, with no AI: tap each arrow on the photo and pick its ring, or tap the scores without a photo
- [x] Manual scoring: mark the target once, so hand-placed arrows count in **Where your arrows group** and the heat map
- [x] Works without signal: photos are saved and scored later, or scored by hand
- [x] Dashboard, history, scoresheet and score-by-end chart
- [x] Bows, with a catalog of current compound bows, draw weight and draw length
- [x] English and French
- [x] German and Spanish, in the app (with the terms and privacy policy translated) and on this website
- [x] Privacy by design: photos trimmed to the target, hidden data removed, nothing about you or your phone sent
- [x] sagitt·AI·re Demo, with six months of sample sessions to try every screen
- [x] Bow types as World Archery's divisions (barebow, recurve, compound), and the discipline of each session (target, field, 3D or para archery)
- [x] Three looks: Original, Range day (for sunlight) and Night range (for indoors), with scores in their ring colours
- [x] Game mode (preview): the hall and the range in pixel art, walk to a place to open it, your own archer, companions, music and sounds, and every screen in the game look; your statistics by the hearth, the end to check at the arrow stand, and levels, badges and trophies earned by real shooting

## Coming

- [x] **Game mode:** outfits and hall decorations earned by levels and badges, a reward for every badge (a quiver, a laurel wreath, a silver cloak, a feathered hat, an owl companion, a painted target and crossed arrows), a war horn for a personal best, and the range in four seasons

- [x] **Your data, your way:** export (everything, records only, or photos only) to a folder you choose, restore on the same or a new phone, and free up space by removing old photos
- [x] Share a session summary, as text or with a picture of the scoresheet
- [x] Statistics: average over time, volume, score distribution, consistency, fatigue over a session, where your arrows group, records per round
- [x] Personal bests per round type (for example 18 m, 60 arrows), on the Dashboard and each session
- [x] Statistics by distance and face, and a heat map per spot
- [x] Smaller markers where arrows are close together
- [x] **Report a scoring problem:** an email about the end, sent to whoever you choose, with the photo only if you add it
- [x] **Sight marks** per bow and distance, shown on the shooting line
- [x] **Arrow sets** and a **tuning log** for each bow, with the tuning changes on the statistics trend
- [x] **Training goals:** arrows per week and an average to reach, on the Dashboard, and a weekly challenge in game mode
- [x] An accessibility check of every screen (large text, screen reader)
- [ ] AI scoring checked on field and 3D faces
