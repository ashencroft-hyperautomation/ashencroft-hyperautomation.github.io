# Look and language

**Settings → Appearance and game** (*Réglages → Apparence et jeu*) sets how Ullr looks and how you get around it.

## Mode

- **Normal**: tabs and screens. See [Normal mode](normal.md).
- **Game (preview)** (*Jeu (aperçu)*): the same app as a pixel-art game, with sound and music you can turn off here. See [Game mode](game.md).

## Theme

In normal mode, three themes:

| Theme | For |
|-------|-----|
| **Original** | The app's original colours, light or dark following the phone. |
| **Range day** (*Jour de tir*) | Light and high-contrast, easy to read in sunlight. Scores in the target's colours. |
| **Night range** (*Tir de nuit*) | Dark with amber, for indoor and evening shooting. Scores in the target's colours. |

Game mode has its own look, on every screen.

## Language

Ullr speaks English and French and follows your phone's language. On Android 13 and later, **Settings → Language** (*Langue*) lets you choose English, French, or the phone's language, for Ullr only.
