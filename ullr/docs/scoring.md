# Three ways to score

Ullr scores an end in one of three ways. You choose in **Settings → Scoring method** (*Réglages → Méthode de comptage*), and you can change at any time: past sessions keep their scores.

![The Scoring method page: what each method does, then the choice between your own API key, sharing with your AI app, and manual.](images/screenshots/settings.png){ .shot width="240" }

| | [Your own API key](scoring-api.md) | [Share with your AI app](scoring-share.md) | [Manual](scoring-manual.md) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In the app** | Your own API key (automatic) (*Votre propre clé API*) | Share with your AI app (*Partager avec votre application d’IA*) | Manual (no AI) (*Manuel (sans IA)*) |
| **You need** | A key from an AI service | An AI app on your phone | Nothing |
| **After the photo** | Scored in the background, ready to check a few seconds later | You share, the AI app answers, you paste the answer | You tap each arrow on the photo and pick its ring |
| **Uncertain arrows circled** | Yes | Yes | You decide every ring |
| **Without signal** | Waits for the network | Needs the AI app's network | Works anywhere |
| **What leaves the phone** | The photo, your setup and your key, to that service only | The photo and the prompt, shared by you | Nothing |
| **Cost** | A few cents per end, on your account with the service | Whatever your AI app costs you | Free |

**Score by hand is always there.** Whatever the method, any end can be scored by hand: **Score by hand instead** (*Saisir à la main*) while waiting, or after a failed scoring.

## Which one?

- **You want it automatic** and don't mind a few cents per end: [your own API key](scoring-api.md).
- **You already pay for an AI chat app** and don't want an API key: [share with your AI app](scoring-share.md).
- **You don't want any AI**, or you shoot where there's no signal: [manual](scoring-manual.md). The photo stays useful: you place your arrows on it, and it keeps the proof.
